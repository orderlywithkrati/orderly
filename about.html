My Story

A small-town girl
with big dreams

Hi! I am Krati Verma. I'm a small-town girl who left home at 16 to chase big dreams. From late-night maggi during IIT-JEE prep in Kota to graduating from IIT Roorkee, one thing stayed constant — my obsession with order. My hostel rooms were famously neat — so much so that people would stop by just to see how everything was arranged! But I didn't realise it was a "thing" until friends started asking me to organise their spaces too.

Post-college, I joined the usual corporate trail, worked with some amazing start-ups, and learned the beauty (and mess) of building from scratch. Eventually, I turned my natural organising instinct into Orderly — launched right from my Bangalore living room, which doubled as my inventory room.

When I'm not organising spaces, you'll find me on a badminton court or experimenting in the kitchen. I love feeding people — it's my love language.

If you're looking to bring calm into your home, want organising systems that work, or just need a nudge to tackle that overflowing closet — you've come to the right place.
